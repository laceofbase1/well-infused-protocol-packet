## What is deliberately not published here

The **formulary** and the **membership quick guide** are internal: they carry clinic pricing.
A GitHub Pages URL is readable by anyone who has the link, so those two are described on the page
but travel as email attachments instead.

## Printing

The prescription pad and Your Protocol are printed properly, on good stock, by a printer.
The formulary and the quick guide are printed at the office, because both change often.
